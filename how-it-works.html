METHODOLOGY & METRICS

How we capture opportunity.

We study the sequence of customer questions, comparison behaviors, and decision stages before they contact a local business.

STAGE 01 // DISCOVER

Find the target queries.

We monitor natural-language questions asked on conversational platforms, compiling the direct queries local consumers ask regarding services, complications, and options.

STAGE 02 // INTERPRET

Isolate buying intent.

Not all questions are commercial. We parse signals to identify the buying stage (Research, Comparison, Decision) and geographical intent limits.

STAGE 03 // POSITION

Build contextual feed answers.

We construct tailored local campaign feeds that answer active questions precisely. This ensures your business matches the searcher's query in context.

STAGE 04 // ACQUIRE

Deliver across conversational channels.

Feeds are syndicated directly to relevant conversational networks, matching placements when a prospective client is making a choice.

STAGE 05 // MEASURE

Track qualified client acquisitions.

We log conversion outcomes. Every call, text, or appointment request is routed through a clean attribution loop back to the initial search query.
