14 / TRANSPARENCY

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do you do?

We identify high-intent questions potential buyers ask AI assistants, build contextual message answers, and publish those feeds to help eligible local businesses acquire them.

Is this the same as traditional Google Ads?

No. Google Ads focuses on matching raw keywords on traditional search result lists. We focus on matching conversational context and user questions where AI synthesizes the answers.

Can any business advertise on ChatGPT?

Advertising eligibility, country availability, and categories are strictly regulated by platform policies. For restricted industries, we focus on organic context feeding and intelligence reports.

Do you guarantee leads?

No. We do not claim guaranteed volumes. Instead, we qualify each lead block against defined service filters to verify real, local customer intent.
